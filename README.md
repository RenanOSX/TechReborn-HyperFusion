# Tech Reborn: Hyper Fusion

*Tech Reborn is a completely standalone tech mod including tools and machines to gather resources, process materials, and progress through the mod.*

**Hyper Fusion** expands upon *Tech Reborn*, introducing new overpowered energy systems, cosmic-scale machines, and technologies capable of bending reality itself.  
Harness the power of advanced energy tiers and solar technologies that transcend known physics.

---

## ☀️ New Solar Panel Line — “The Hyper Series”

The Hyper Fusion update adds **8 new solar panel tiers**, each representing a step closer to total energy control:

- **Spectral** – Captures the full solar spectrum, including ultraviolet and infrared wavelengths.  
- **Photonic** – Manipulates individual photons for maximum energy conversion efficiency.  
- **Diffractive** – Channels light through diffraction and interference to generate focused energy.  
- **Protonic** – Extracts power from low-scale protonic reactions.  
- **Neutron** – Produces stable energy via controlled neutron reactions.  
- **Baryon** – Taps into dense baryonic energy stored in protons and neutrons.  
- **Hadron** – Unlocks confinement energy within hadronic structures.  
- **Quark** – Accesses the ultimate potential of quark–gluon interactions.

Each tier pushes technology beyond conventional science — from subatomic fusion to quantum manipulation.

---
